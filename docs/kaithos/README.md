# Kaithos

**A turn-based game of myth, strategy, and the golden spiral. One turn is one day.
You build, you scheme, you betray, you ascend — and every season the cosmos unwinds anew.**

> 🔒 **Private · pre-launch · work in progress.** This repository is closed until launch.

---

## What Kaithos is

Kaithos is a **daily-turn persistent strategy game** in the lineage of the great browser
"kingdom war" games — rebuilt, top to bottom, on the **golden ratio** and the esoteric
myth that the cosmos is made of proportion.

- **One tick = one day.** Asynchronous. Respects real life. Plan with your kingdom the
  night before; the world resolves at dawn.
- **You are a hero** — with a town, an army, and a network of spies — inside a **kingdom**
  (your team, your friends).
- **A season is a real season** — three months, four a year, Spring → Summer → Autumn →
  Winter. Built to run for years.
- **You win as a kingdom *and* as an individual**, across many rankings at once.

The name fuses *Cait* and *Mythos*, and echoes the star **Kaitos**.
