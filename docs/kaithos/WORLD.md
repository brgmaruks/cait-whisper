# Kaithos — The World

**A turn-based game of myth, strategy, and the golden spiral. One turn is one day.
You build, you scheme, you betray, you ascend — and every season the cosmos unwinds anew.**

## What Kaithos is  **[LOCKED]**

Kaithos is a **daily-turn persistent strategy game** in the lineage of the great
browser "kingdom war" games — but built, top to bottom, on the **golden ratio** and the
esoteric myth that the cosmos is made of proportion.

- **One tick = one day.** Asynchronous. Respects real life. You log in, set your orders,
  and the world resolves at dawn. The magic is in the planning, not the reflexes.
- **You are a hero, with a town, an army, and a network of spies**, inside a **kingdom**
  (your team — your friends).
- **A season is a real season** — three months, four a year, riding Spring → Summer →
  Autumn → Winter. Kaithos is built to run for years.
- **You win as a kingdom *and* as an individual**, across many rankings at once.

It lives inside the CAIT ecosystem as a CaitOS module, with its own token (**$KAI**)
mined through play. The name fuses *Cait* and *Mythos*, and echoes the star **Kaitos**.

## The Cosmology — *all is Proportion*  **[LOCKED]**

> In the beginning is **the Monad** — the One, undivided, the source. It does not split
> in half. It splits by the **golden cut** — the one division where the smaller part is
> to the larger as the larger is to the whole. From that first sacred wound, creation
> **spirals outward**: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13… each age born from the sum of the two
> before it. Gods, then titans, then heroes, then mortals — each an unfolding whorl of
> the same spiral. This is **the Proportion**, and everything that lives is a note in
> its music.

φ is not decoration here — it is the **creation myth, the physics, and the religion** at
once. *As above, so below.* When a building costs a Fibonacci number, that is the cosmos
obeying its own law, not a UI quirk.

This is the **perennial philosophy** made playable: every old pantheon — Olympian,
Orphic, Egyptian, Norse, Vedic — is a **facet of the One Proportion**, a different
culture glimpsing the same spiral.

## Kingdoms & Factions — syncretic pantheons  **[LOCKED]**

A kingdom is a band of players (your friends) *and* a **mystery-tradition**. Each
pantheon is a different edge of the One, and grants a subtle **lean** — flavor and
asymmetry, never a power imbalance. *Illustrative* leans (to be tuned):

| Tradition | Leans toward | Flavor |
|---|---|---|
| Olympian | Balanced / Force | Order, the phalanx, open war |
| Orphic | Subtlety (Espionage) | Secrets, the underworld road, initiates |
| Egyptian | Power (Hero / Aether) | Death, rebirth, divine proportion channeled |
| Norse | Force | Raiding, fury, the long winter |
| Vedic | Power / Economy | Cycles, cosmic order, abundance |

"Which tradition are you running this season?" is a real, replayable choice. *All facets
of the One; each sees a different edge of the spiral.*

## The Map — the spiral that is also the climax  **[LOCKED]**

The board is a **phyllotaxis spiral**: provinces sown like sunflower seeds, each at the
**golden angle (137.5°)** from the last, radiating from the center — **the Monad / the
Eye**. Players begin on the wide outer whorls; the Eye sits empty and sacred at the
heart.

**Adjacency runs along golden spiral arms.** In a real sunflower the seeds form visible
spiral *arms*, and the number of arms is always a pair of consecutive **Fibonacci
numbers** (13 & 21, or 21 & 34). So who you can *reach* — attack, scout, ally with — is
not a grid neighborhood: **your reachable neighbors are your index ± a Fibonacci
number.** Your front line is a *spiral*, not a wall. Alliances form *along the arms*;
pincers come from up and down the whorl. It is a spatial logic no grid game has — and it
falls straight out of φ.

## The Season Arc — Emanation, Contraction, Convergence  **[LOCKED]**

A season (~90 ticks) is a three-movement story, and the geometry itself forces the
climax — no arbitrary rules required.

1. **Emanation** *(early — the wide rim).* Space is vast. Build economy, stake
   territory, kingdoms coalesce along the arms, light raiding tests borders.

2. **Contraction** *(mid).* The **Dissolution** — the entropy at the edge of creation —
   begins eating inward from the rim. Each period the outermost whorl becomes
   uninhabitable. You are **pushed inward** (you *retreat and consolidate*, not die — you
   only fall if you lose every province, so friends stay in the game). Territory grows
   scarce; skirmishes become real wars.

3. **Convergence** *(climax — the inner whorls).* Only the heart remains. Every surviving
   kingdom is crushed together around the Eye. Confrontation is not a rule — **there is
   nowhere else to be.**

**Two forces pull everyone to the center**, so the climax is structural:
- **Spatial** — the map literally shrinks inward.
- **Economic** — **Aether**, the rare resource that fuels Hero/magic, pools near the
  Monad. Holding the center *pays* — so the bold push in early, exposed and surrounded.

At the collapse, the spiral folds into the Monad; whoever holds the **Eye** "ascends" and
is inscribed in the **Hall of Fame** forever. Then the world dissolves and, with the next
real-world season, **the spiral unwinds anew from the One.**

> If some cunning soul games the Dissolution and turtles on the rim through the collapse
> — **good for them.** That becomes a legend the chain remembers. We design the squeeze;
> we don't paranoidly police the edges.

## The Five Elements  **[LOCKED]**

A pentagram of resources, mapped to the wheel of the year:

| Element | Season | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Air** | Spring | Waxes in spring, wanes in autumn |
| **Fire** | Summer | Waxes in summer, wanes in winter |
| **Earth** | Autumn | Waxes in autumn, wanes in spring |
| **Water** | Winter | Waxes in winter, wanes in summer |
| **Aether** | *eternal / center* | The rare quintessence — never seasonal, pooled at the Monad; fuels Hero / magic |

Each game-season one element waxes abundant and its opposite wanes, so the economy
**shifts** every season — what you hoard in summer is scarce by winter. **Aether** is the
still point of the turning wheel: always rare, concentrated at the center, the reason to
brave the Eye. All costs climb the **Fibonacci ladder**.

## The Four Pillars of Power  **[LOCKED]**

Everything you do flows from four systems that **compete for the same finite daily
budget** — every day spent on one is a day not spent on the others.

- **Town** — your economy. Buildings, population, resource generation. Your foundation.
- **Hero** — *you*. Levels, skills, a build identity. The throughline across seasons —
  the hero carries **reputation, not power** (see below).
- **Units** — your military, trained from population; the force you project.
- **Espionage** — your eyes and your knife in the dark. Scout before you strike;
  sabotage instead of marching.

## The Offensive Triangle — the sacred forms  **[LOCKED]**

Three paths to power, each a Pythagorean symbol, each countering another so no single
strategy dominates and **intel always matters**:

| Path | Symbol | Counters |
|---|---|---|
| **Force** (army) | the **Square** — earth, the phalanx | beats **Power** (steel grounds the ethereal) |
| **Subtlety** (espionage) | the **Pentagram** — the initiates' secret sign | beats **Force** (the unseen knife undoes the phalanx) |
| **Power** (hero / magic) | the **Spiral / Dodecahedron** — aether, proportion channeled | beats **Subtlety** (divine sight pierces secrecy) |

You spy *to learn which way a rival leans* before committing your day's orders into the
dark.

## The Daily Loop  **[DRAFT — active thread]**

The heartbeat, and the next thing we're nailing down. Working model:

- At dawn the tick resolves and you receive a **budget of action-points** for the day —
  **Fibonacci-scaled** to your development, not a real-time energy bar (so sleeping never
  punishes you).
- You spend the budget on **orders**: **Build · Train · Scout · Move · Strike** (and
  diplomacy / sabotage).
- Orders are **committed in secret** and **resolve simultaneously** for everyone on the
  next tick. Nobody reacts in real time; everybody commits in the dark and finds out at
  dawn. Chess by mail — your kingdom plots the night before in the group chat.

*Open questions:* exact action-point economy, how Move interacts with the spiral arms,
fog-of-war rules, and how combat resolution reads the offensive triangle. **← we riff
here next.**

## Reputation, Heroes & Betrayal  **[LOCKED]**

- The **hero persists across seasons** in your wallet — accruing **reputation and
  history** (titles won, kingdoms led), never raw power. In-season power resets every
  season; everyone starts a season dead equal.
- **Betrayal is allowed.** You *can* knife your own kingdom or its treasury — but it is
  **inscribed in the Hall of Fame forever.** Treachery is a weighty, reputation-staking
  choice that makes legends, not a cheap exploit.

## Rankings — many ladders, not one  **[LOCKED]**

Everyone gets to be *the best at something*, which is what keeps the mid-pack logging in:

- **Top Kingdom** (the headline collective prize)
- **Top Individual** (overall)
- **Top Warrior** (most successful attacks)
- **Top Spy** (most successful ops / least caught)
- **Top Economy** (greatest dominion)
- **Fastest Riser** · **Greatest Battle** · and more

Rankings settle into **Fibonacci-sized tiers** (top 1, then 2, 3, 5, 8, 13…), and pay out
via the [Golden Distribution](TOKENOMICS.md#the-golden-distribution).

## Design Principles  **[LOCKED]**

- **Proof of play, not pay-to-win.** Skill and coordination win. Money buys vanity only.
- **The math is diegetic.** φ is the world's physics, not a gimmick laid on top.
- **Asynchronous and social.** Respect real life; reward the group chat.
- **Fair within a cohort, scarce across time.** Every season is winnable by newcomers.
- **Force the climax, don't police the edges.** The cosmos ends each season; if someone
  outwits the ending, that's a legend, not a bug to crush.
