cait. whisper
=============

Free, fully local voice-to-text for Windows. Your voice never leaves your PC.


HOW TO START
------------

1. Double-click  "Cait Whisper"  (in this folder).
   This is the only thing you ever click - first time and every time after.

2. The first time, Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box.
   This is just because the app isn't code-signed yet (signing costs money).
   Click  "More info"  ->  "Run anyway".  It is safe.

3. Click  "Yes"  on the permission prompt. (Cait Whisper needs this so its
   keyboard shortcut works in every app.)

4. A small coral coin appears at the bottom-center of your screen, just above
   the taskbar. The first launch downloads the speech model (needs internet,
   about a minute). After that it's instant and works offline.


HOW TO USE
----------

  - Click into any text box (email, chat, document, browser).
  - HOLD  Ctrl + Windows key , speak, then RELEASE.
  - Your words appear where your cursor is.

  Right-click the coin for everything else: switch models, change where it
  sits, open History & Settings, and more.


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------

  - Nothing happens when you hold Ctrl+Win:
      Make sure you clicked "Yes" on the permission prompt at startup.

  - It can't hear you:
      Right-click the coin -> Microphone -> pick the right input device.

  - Something else:
      Open  cait-whisper.log  (in this folder) - it records what went wrong.


Keep this whole folder together. To move the app, move the folder. To
uninstall, just delete it.

More help:  https://github.com/brgmaruks/cait-whisper
